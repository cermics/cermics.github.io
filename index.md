<p style="text-align: justify;">    
CERMICS
is the research center in applied mathematics of <a
href="http://www.enpc.fr/en" target="_blank">&Eacute;cole nationale des ponts et
chaussées</a>. It is a joint research unit with <a href="https://www.cnrs.fr/en"
target="_blank">CNRS</a> (UMR 9032). It hosts joint project-teams with <a
href="https://www.inria.fr/en/centre/paris" target="_blank">Inria</a> <a
href="https://www.inria.fr/en/centre/paris" target="_blank"> Paris</a> and <a
href="https://www.univ-gustave-eiffel.fr/en/" target="_blank">University Gustave
Eiffel</a>, and is part of the <a
href="https://bezout.univ-paris-est.fr/bezout-federation/">Bézout CNRS
Federation</a> and the doctoral school <a
href="https://www.fondation-hadamard.fr/en/campus-paris-saclay/training/edmh/"
target="_blank">EDMH</a>. The acronym CERMICS stands for “Centre d’Enseignement
et de Recherche en MathématIques et Calcul Scientifique”, literally “Center for
Training and Research in MathematIcs and Scientific Computing”. 
</p>
